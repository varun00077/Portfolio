About Me

I am an undergraduate student at IIIT Bhagalpur with a strong interest in Computer Vision and Representation Learning. My current focus is on understanding and modeling subtle temporal patterns in videos, particularly for facial micro-expression recognition.

I have worked on research-oriented projects involving optical flow, temporal modeling, and deep learning architectures, with hands-on experience evaluating models on benchmark micro-expression datasets using standard evaluation protocols.

I am actively looking to further strengthen my research background through internships and collaborations in computer vision and machine learning.
